    About Our Product

    Eagles Health Simulation delivers clinical training technologies for medical schools, nursing colleges, and emergency response academies. By combinig life-like robotic mannequins with AI-driven diagnostic software, we create realistic medical environment for zero-risk clinical practice.

    health simulation

    Immersive Biomedical & Healthcare Traininig

    Bring advanced medical technology into classrooms. Eagles Health Simulation integrates AI diagnostics, biomedical robotics, and Extended Reality (AR/VR) to train the next generation of healthcare professionals and biomedical engineers.

    Core Simulation Hardware & Software

    1. High-Fidelity Patient Manikins

    • Responsive airways, realistic pulse points, and dynamic pupil dilations.
    • Simulated heart rate, blood pressure, and lung sound variations controllable via instructor tablet.
    • Automated physiological responses to drug dosages and IV fluid adminstration.

    2. AI Diagnostic Scenario Engine

    • Hundreds of pre-built patient case studies covering cardiology, trauma, pediatrics, and neurology.
    • Real-time student performance scoring based on diagnostics accuracy and time-to-treatment.
    • Adaptive AI patients that articulate symptoms dynamically during verbal examinations.

    3. Surgical & Procedural Task Trainers

    • Tactile training modules for suture practice, IV insertion, catheterization, and laparoscopy.
    • Integrated force-feedback sensors that alert trainees to tissue damage or improper force.

    Robotics Lab Establishment

    Turn Any Room into a World-Class STEM & Innovation Hub.

    Eagles Tech designs, equips, and sets up state-of-the-art AI and Robotics Labs for schools, universities, and training centers. We handle everything from hardware and software integration to curriculum deployment and teacher onboarding.

    What We Provide
    • Turnkey Infrastructure: High-performance workstations, Eagles Smart Screens, safety gear, and modular laboratory furniture.
    • Hardware & Robotics Kits: Complete sets of microcontrollers, sensors, autonomous rover kits, robotic arms, and IoT development boards.
    • Curriculum & Courseware: Grade-appropriate AI, Python coding, and robotics modules paired with Eagles Tech textbooks and interactive guides.
    • Teacher Training & Certification: Comprehensive workshops to empower educators with the technical skills to run and maintain the lab effortlessly.
    Establishment Process in 4 Steps
    • Space & Needs Assessment: We analyze your school's space, budget, and learning objectives.
    • Lab Design & Setup: Installation of hardware, Smart Screens, networks, and safety infrastructure.
    • Software & Curriculum Deployment: Loading EXR simulation software, AI platforms, and learning portals.
    • Educator Onboarding: Hands-on training for staff and full operational launch support.

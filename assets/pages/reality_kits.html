    About Our Product

    Eagles Extended Reality (EXR) is an immersive educational platform that transforms traditional STEM learning into interactive, 3D experinces. By pairing our physical textbooks and robotics kits with AR, VR, and Mixed Reality (MR), students don't just read about technology-they interact with it.

    Eagles Extended Reality.

    Bridging theory and practice. Eagles Extended Reality (EXR) pairs our AI / robotics textbooks and hardware kits with immersive 3D, AR, and VR learning modules.

    AI Algorithm Visualizer

    Watch neural networks, computer vision pathways, and machine learning models process data in real-time within a 3D virtual environment. Sign up in 30 seconds. Set up your first project and invite your team with one link.

    AR-Enhanced Textbooks

    Scan pages in the your Eagles Tech textbooks using the EXR Mobile App to bring diagrams, circuit schematics, and structural models to life.

    Virtual Robotics Lab & Simulation

    Test code and simulate robot movements in realistic, physics-enabled virtual environments before uploading scripts to physical hardware.

    Interactive 3D Hardware Disassembly

    Zoom in, rotate, and inspect complex robotics components, microcontrollers, and sensor arrays down to the micro-level before building your physical kits
